Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-11

Stopwatch Scheduler is a personal, single-user application. It has one user — its developer — and is not offered as a public service or product. This policy exists to describe how the app handles data, including data accessed through Google's APIs.

What data the app accesses

With the developer's own Google account authorization, the app reads and writes Google Calendar events in order to:

  • Display the developer's existing Google Calendar events alongside app-tracked activities
  • Create Google Calendar events from recordings and schedules built in the app

The app also stores its own data — recorded activity durations, schedules, and related metadata — in a private database that only the developer can access.

How data is stored

All data, including the Google OAuth credentials used to access Calendar, is stored on infrastructure controlled solely by the developer. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party for any purpose, including advertising.

Revoking access

Google Calendar access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
