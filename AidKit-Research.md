# AidKit: Deep Dive Research Summary

## Company Overview

**What is AidKit?**
AidKit is a Certified B Corporation and Public Benefit Corporation founded in 2021 that provides cutting-edge technology platforms and services to help governments and nonprofits deliver aid, benefits, and relief programs with speed, fairness, and effectiveness.

**Origin Story**
AidKit was born out of the Left Behind Workers Fund, a pandemic relief initiative in Colorado during COVID-19. When existing technology couldn't meet the program's accessibility and data privacy requirements, the founders built a customizable cash delivery platform. This emergency solution evolved into permanent infrastructure for aid distribution that now serves over 240 government and nonprofit organizations.

**Scale & Impact (as of 2025-2026)**
- Serves 240+ government agencies and nonprofit organizations
- Processed 600,000+ applications
- Distributed $385 million in aid
- Impacted an estimated 280,000+ people (individuals, households, organizations, and small businesses)
- Programs launch in days rather than months
- Significant cost savings compared to traditional consulting models

**Recent Milestones**
- **March 2025**: Raised $8.5 million in Series A growth equity financing led by Blueprint Equity
- **April 2025**: Launched AidKit Essentials, an end-to-end platform for rapid aid program deployment
- **2025 Awards**: Stevie Award for Technology Excellence; Gold Globee® Award for Impact (AI in identity verification, fraud detection, and accessibility)
- **2026**: Entered the year as permanent infrastructure for fast, fair, and scalable aid with new brand identity

**Primary Mission Statement**
AidKit builds human-centered technology to help governments and nonprofits deliver aid with dignity, transparency, and impact. Their broader mission is to free people from the reinforcing traps of poverty by creating effective, efficient, and dignified aid experiences.
